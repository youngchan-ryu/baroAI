The data was extracted from Geofabrik's South Korea OSM snapshot of 2026-11-14: https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/south-korea.html. It has been processed into WGS84 GeoJSON containing selected building outlines, roads and paths for five campus bounding boxes. Bounding-box intersecting ways are included whole and can extend well beyond the intended viewport. The data can be incomplete or out of date and is not a surveyed or verified building-location dataset. No OSM tile images are included.

Source, reproducibility and checksums: data/campus-profile/map-source/README.md in the project source distribution. Processing script: scripts/build_campus_basemaps.py.
